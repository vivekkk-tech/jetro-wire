RBI Keeps Interest Rates at 5.25%; Expects India's Economy to Grow Faster at 6.7%

Sources: Reserve Bank of India ↗ • Economic Times ↗ • Moneycontrol ↗

What happened? The Reserve Bank of India (RBI) decided today to keep the country's main interest rate (repo rate) unchanged at 5.25%. This is the 4th time in a row they have kept rates steady.

Why does this matter in simple terms? When interest rates don't increase, loans for homes, cars, and businesses stay affordable. RBI Governor Sanjay Malhotra also said India's economy is doing great and increased India's expected growth rate to 6.7% for this financial year.

💡 WHAT THIS MEANS FOR YOU
  • Home Loan EMIs: Your monthly EMI payments will not increase.
  • Bank Shares: Stocks of HDFC Bank, ICICI Bank, and SBI went up because people will keep taking loans.
